## 🎯 What You Can Do Right Now

1. **Send Email to Selected Contacts** ✅
   - Click checkboxes to select
   - Click "Send Email" button
   - Done!

2. **Send Followup Emails** ✅
   - Select contacts
   - Click "Send Followup"
   - Done!

3. **Start Email Sequences** ✅
   - Select 5+ contacts
   - Click "Start Sequence"
   - Choose A or B (A/B testing)
   - Emails send automatically over 18 days

4. **Run Scheduled Sends** ✅
   - Click "Run Scheduled"
   - All due emails send
   - Done!

5. **Upload Contacts** ✅
   - Click "Import CSV"
   - Select file
   - Done!

6. **View Statistics** ✅
   - Top of page shows:
     - Total Contacts
     - Emails Sent
     - Opened
     - Replies
