================================================================================
                        WHAT EACH BUTTON DOES
================================================================================

RUN SCHEDULED SENDS (Blue ⚡)
  └─ Sends all emails that are due based on schedule
  └─ Triggered immediately (not waiting for cron)
  └─ Good for: Manual triggering of scheduled emails

SEND EMAIL (Green ✉️)
  └─ Sends email immediately to selected contacts
  └─ Bypasses any schedule
  └─ Good for: Cold outreach to new contacts

SEND FOLLOWUP (Purple ✉️)
  └─ Sends followup email to selected contacts
  └─ Different email variant
  └─ Good for: Following up on non-responders

START SEQUENCE (Orange ▶️)
  └─ Initializes 5-email automated sequence
  └─ Runs for 18 days automatically
  └─ Choose between A (US) or B (UK)
  └─ Good for: Long-term engagement
