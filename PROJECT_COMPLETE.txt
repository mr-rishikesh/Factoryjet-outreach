================================================================================
                        COMMON QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q: How do I send an email?
A: Select 1+ contacts, click "Send Email" button, done!

Q: How do I start sequences?
A: Select 5+ contacts, click "Start Sequence", choose A or B, done!

Q: Where are my stats?
A: Top of dashboard - "Total Contacts, Emails Sent, Opened, Replies"

Q: Can I edit emails before sending?
A: No - generated by Groq AI. Adjust prompt in code if needed.

Q: What if something breaks?
A: Check browser console (F12) and server logs. Read TESTING_CHECKLIST.md

Q: How do I upload contacts?
A: Click "Import CSV" button, select file, upload!

Q: Where's the data stored?
A: MongoDB (running locally on port 27017)

Q: Can I use this in production?
A: Yes! After adding authentication and SSL. See docs.
