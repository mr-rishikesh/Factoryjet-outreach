SEQUENCE RULES (Read Before Sending Anything)
Based on 2026 benchmark data — Instantly, Hunter.io, Autobound — 31M+ emails analysed:

Under 80 words for Email 1. Under 120 for Emails 2–4. Email 5 under 60.
One CTA per email. Multiple asks kill reply rates.
Plain text only. No images, no HTML, no tracking pixels. Campaigns without open tracking see +68% higher reply rates.
Send Tuesday–Wednesday, 7–11am recipient's local time.
Never start with "I". Never say "just checking in", "hope this finds you well", "following up on my last email".
Each follow-up = new angle. Not a bump. Not a repeat. Not "did you see my last email?"
Spacing: Day 1 → Day 4 → Day 8 → Day 13 → Day 19.
Target list size per batch: 20–50 prospects. Smaller and tighter = higher reply rates.
Bounce rate: Keep below 2%. Verify every email address before uploading.
Unsubscribe link: Required from Email 2 onward for CAN-SPAM (US). From Email 1 for UK.

Personalization rule: Only personalise what you can verify in under 30 seconds per prospect. Badly filled tokens are worse than no personalisation. The three safe tokens for every email are:

{{first_name}} — prospect's first name
{{company}} — their brand or company name
{{industry}} — what vertical they operate in (skincare, supplements, apparel, SaaS, etc.) — visible on their website in seconds

Everything else is optional and should only be added when you can verify it with certainty.

WORDS AND PHRASES TO NEVER USE
The following trigger spam filters in 2026, specifically from non-Western infrastructure. Remove from every subject line and body:

❌ Free, guarantee, no risk, risk-free ❌ Limited time, act now, urgent, important ❌ Click here, visit our website, check this out ❌ SEO, search engine ranking, Google ranking ❌ Discount, offer, deal, savings, affordable ❌ Passive income, revenue streams, earn money ❌ I hope this finds you well ❌ Just checking in / just following up ❌ Per my last email / as mentioned ❌ We are a leading agency / we are the best ❌ Revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, innovative ❌ Excited to connect / thrilled to reach out / delighted ❌ Exclamation marks in any subject line ❌ ALL CAPS anywhere in subject or body ❌ Multiple links in a single email ❌ Attachments in Emails 1 or 2
